Fifteen questions to argue over

Print them at home, five sheets, three to an A4 sheet. There is no sign-up, no email, and nothing to accept.

Download all fifteen (PDF)

  1. carry a bag that always smells of one old banana …or… sit in a room that always smells of one wet dog
  2. put your hand in a pocket and find something you forgot …or… put it in and find something you did not put there
  3. smell faintly of onions to everyone but yourself …or… smell wonderful but only to insects
  4. bite something that crunches when it should not …or… bite something that squeaks when it should not
  5. drink something warm that should be cold …or… drink something lumpy that should be smooth
  6. have one tooth that whistles …or… have one finger that clicks every time you point
  7. sit on a chair somebody has just left …or… sit on a chair that is inexplicably cold
  8. have a cough that sounds like a duck …or… have a sneeze that sets off car alarms
  9. find a hair in every meal you did not cook …or… find a crumb in every bed you did not make
  10. have feet that leave footprints indoors …or… have hands that leave fingerprints on everything
  11. eat a biscuit that tastes faintly of soap …or… eat soup that tastes faintly of biscuit
  12. have a fridge that hums a tune you hate …or… have a fridge that smells of last Tuesday
  13. wear a jumper full of static for a day …or… wear trousers that rustle very loudly
  14. have breath that fogs mirrors in summer …or… have hands that fog glasses in winter
  15. be followed everywhere by one small fly that likes you …or… be avoided by every animal that has ever met you

Written for this page, not taken from a book

These fifteen appear in none of the books. They were written to be given away, which is the whole of it — and every one has two answers somebody could defend, because a question with an obvious answer is a dead question.
