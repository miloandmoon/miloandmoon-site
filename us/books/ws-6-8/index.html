A hundred word searches, in four levels

Level 1 hides words across and down only. Each level after it adds one new direction and nothing else, so the book gets harder in steps a child can see coming — and every page says which level it is on.

  • 100 puzzles, one to a page, in big clear print
  • Four levels, from across-and-down to all eight directions
  • Every page names its level and repeats that level's rule
  • A line to write the time taken, on every puzzle
  • Answers free online — no sign-up, no email, nothing to buy
