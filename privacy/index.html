Privacy Policy

The short version: this website collects nothing about you at all. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no forms.

Who we are

This is the privacy policy for miloandmoon.co.uk, operated by Milo & Moon Ltd, an independent publisher of children's colouring story books registered in England and Wales, company number 17391753. You can reach us at hello@miloandmoon.co.uk.

This website

We do not use cookies. We do not use analytics. Every image, style and font on this site is served from this domain, so loading a page here does not tell any other company that you visited. There are no forms, no sign-ups and no accounts, so there is nothing for us to store.

Our web host keeps standard server logs, which may include IP addresses, as every web server does. We do not have access to them and do not use them.

If you email us

We keep your message and address so we can reply, and for as long as it is useful to have the conversation on record. We will not add you to a mailing list, we do not have one, and we will never pass your details to anyone else. Ask us to delete your email and we will.

Social media

We post on Pinterest, Facebook and Instagram. Those services set their own cookies and collect their own data under their own policies, which we do not control. Our tools publish our own pictures to our own accounts and read back our own statistics — they do not collect anything about the people who see them.

Children

Our books are for children aged 4 to 8. This website, and everything we publish, is addressed to the adult buying them. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our holding it. Email us and we will do it. If you are unhappy with how we respond you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this ever stops being true, we will change it here. Last updated 27 August 2026.
